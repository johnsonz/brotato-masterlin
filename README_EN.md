# Brotato Mod Master Lin (林教头)

[中文](README.md) | **English** 

***Don’t mistake Lin Daiyu for frail and slight, She’ll yank a willow tree with all her might!***

Adds brand-new characters, weapons, items and pets to Brotato. Every character comes with unique custom mechanics and a signature weapon or item.
